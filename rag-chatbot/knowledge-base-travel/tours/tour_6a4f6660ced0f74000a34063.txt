TOUR: Causeway Bay - Hong Kong

Description:
Causeway Bay, Hong Kong — a 5-day journey focused on architecture, local culture, nature and beaches.

Causeway Bay is a popular shopping and entertainment district, home to large department stores, trendy boutiques, and a vibrant food scene.

Across 5 days you will visit Times Square , Victoria Park  and Hysan Place. The pace is deliberately relaxed, with unhurried mornings and plenty of free time.
Expect a few hours of walking each day, including some uneven ground and stairs. The minimum age for this departure is 6, and groups are capped at 22 travellers so the guide can give everyone attention.

Food is part of the experience: you will have the chance to try Wonton Noodles, Hotpot and Bubble Tea. Breakfast is served at the hotel every morning, and several lunches are included while you are out with the guide.

The best months to travel are November, December and January, when the weather suits the activities in this itinerary. The price of 4200 USD per adult covers accommodation, transport on the ground, an English-speaking guide and entrance fees to the sites listed above.

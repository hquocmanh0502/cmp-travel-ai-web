TOUR: Amsterdam - Netherlands

Description:
Amsterdam, Netherlands — a 3-day journey focused on museums, history, food and great value.

Amsterdam is the capital of the Netherlands, known for its picturesque canals, historic architecture, and vibrant cultural scene.

Across 3 days you will visit Rijksmuseum, Van Gogh Museum and Anne Frank House. The pace is full, designed for travellers who want to see as much as possible.
Most days involve light walking on flat ground, so no special fitness is required. The minimum age for this departure is 0, and groups are capped at 23 travellers so the guide can give everyone attention.

Food is part of the experience: you will have the chance to try Stroopwafel, Poffertjes and Herring. Breakfast is served at the hotel every morning, and several lunches are included while you are out with the guide.

The best months to travel are April, May and June, when the weather suits the activities in this itinerary. The price of 4000 USD per adult covers accommodation, transport on the ground, an English-speaking guide and entrance fees to the sites listed above.

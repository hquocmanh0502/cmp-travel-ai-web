TOUR: Munich - Germany

Description:
Munich, Germany — a 5-day journey focused on history, nature, food and premium comfort.

Munich, the capital of Bavaria, is famous for its Oktoberfest celebrations, stunning architecture, and vibrant beer gardens.

Across 5 days you will visit Marienplatz, Nymphenburg Palace and English Garden. The pace is balanced: guided highlights in the morning, free time in the afternoon.
Expect a few hours of walking each day, including some uneven ground and stairs. The minimum age for this departure is 6, and groups are capped at 12 travellers so the guide can give everyone attention.

Food is part of the experience: you will have the chance to try Pretzels, Weisswurst and Apfelstrudel. Breakfast is served at the hotel every morning, and several lunches are included while you are out with the guide.

The best months to travel are April, May and June, when the weather suits the activities in this itinerary. The price of 5500 USD per adult covers accommodation, transport on the ground, an English-speaking guide and entrance fees to the sites listed above.

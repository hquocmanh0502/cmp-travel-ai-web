TOUR: Phuket - Thailand

Description:
Phuket, Thailand — a 7-day journey focused on beaches, island life, nightlife and food.

Phuket, an island province in southern Thailand, is renowned for its stunning beaches, clear turquoise waters, and vibrant nightlife.

Across 7 days you will visit Patong Beach, Phi Phi Islands and Big Buddha. The pace is deliberately relaxed, with unhurried mornings and plenty of free time.
Most days involve light walking on flat ground, so no special fitness is required. The minimum age for this departure is 0, and groups are capped at 12 travellers so the guide can give everyone attention.

Food is part of the experience: you will have the chance to try Tom Kha Gai, Massaman Curry and Mango with Sticky Rice. Breakfast is served at the hotel every morning, and several lunches are included while you are out with the guide.

The best months to travel are November, December and January, when the weather suits the activities in this itinerary. The price of 6000 USD per adult covers accommodation, transport on the ground, an English-speaking guide and entrance fees to the sites listed above.

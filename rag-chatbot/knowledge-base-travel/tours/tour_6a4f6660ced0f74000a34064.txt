TOUR: Athens - Greece

Description:
Athens, Greece — a 5-day journey focused on history, architecture, local culture and wellness.

Athens, the ancient capital, is a city steeped in history, showcasing iconic ruins, ancient temples, and a rich cultural heritage.

Across 5 days you will visit Acropolis, Parthenon and Plaka. The pace is deliberately relaxed, with unhurried mornings and plenty of free time.
Most days involve light walking on flat ground, so no special fitness is required. The minimum age for this departure is 0, and groups are capped at 24 travellers so the guide can give everyone attention.

Food is part of the experience: you will have the chance to try Moussaka, Souvlaki and Spanakopita. Breakfast is served at the hotel every morning, and several lunches are included while you are out with the guide.

The best months to travel are April, May and June, when the weather suits the activities in this itinerary. The price of 4500 USD per adult covers accommodation, transport on the ground, an English-speaking guide and entrance fees to the sites listed above.

TOUR: Montreal - Canada

Description:
Montreal, Canada — a 3-day journey focused on museums, history, local culture and nature.

Montreal is a vibrant city with a strong French influence, known for its historic architecture, artsy neighborhoods, and lively festivals.

Across 3 days you will visit Old Montreal, Montreal Museum of Fine Arts and Mount Royal Park. The pace is full, designed for travellers who want to see as much as possible.
Expect a few hours of walking each day, including some uneven ground and stairs. The minimum age for this departure is 6, and groups are capped at 20 travellers so the guide can give everyone attention.

Food is part of the experience: you will have the chance to try Bagels, Poutine and Smoked Meat Sandwich. Breakfast is served at the hotel every morning, and several lunches are included while you are out with the guide.

The best months to travel are June, July and August, when the weather suits the activities in this itinerary. The price of 4000 USD per adult covers accommodation, transport on the ground, an English-speaking guide and entrance fees to the sites listed above.

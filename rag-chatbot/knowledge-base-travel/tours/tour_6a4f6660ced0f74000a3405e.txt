TOUR: Moscow - Russia

Description:
Moscow, Russia — a 5-day journey focused on history, architecture, food and great value.

Moscow is the capital city of Russia, known for its iconic landmarks, rich history, and vibrant cultural scene.

Across 5 days you will visit Red Square, Kremlin and St. Basil's Cathedral and Bolshoi Theatre. The pace is balanced: guided highlights in the morning, free time in the afternoon.
Most days involve light walking on flat ground, so no special fitness is required. The minimum age for this departure is 0, and groups are capped at 24 travellers so the guide can give everyone attention.

Food is part of the experience: you will have the chance to try Borscht, Pelmeni and Blini. Breakfast is served at the hotel every morning, and several lunches are included while you are out with the guide.

The best months to travel are May, June and July, when the weather suits the activities in this itinerary. The price of 5000 USD per adult covers accommodation, transport on the ground, an English-speaking guide and entrance fees to the sites listed above.

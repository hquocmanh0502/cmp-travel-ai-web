TOUR: Osaka - Japan

Description:
Osaka, Japan — a 5-day journey focused on history, shopping, nightlife and food.

Osaka is a lively city known for its delicious street food, modern architecture, and vibrant nightlife.

Across 5 days you will visit Osaka Castle, Dotonbori and Shinsaibashi Shopping Street. The pace is balanced: guided highlights in the morning, free time in the afternoon.
Most days involve light walking on flat ground, so no special fitness is required. The minimum age for this departure is 0, and groups are capped at 21 travellers so the guide can give everyone attention.

Food is part of the experience: you will have the chance to try Takoyaki, Okonomiyaki and Kushikatsu. Breakfast is served at the hotel every morning, and several lunches are included while you are out with the guide.

The best months to travel are March, April and May, when the weather suits the activities in this itinerary. The price of 4200 USD per adult covers accommodation, transport on the ground, an English-speaking guide and entrance fees to the sites listed above.

TOUR: Bangkok - Thailand

Description:
Bangkok, Thailand — a 7-day journey focused on history, architecture, shopping and food.

Bangkok, the capital city of Thailand, is a vibrant metropolis known for its ornate temples, bustling markets, and vibrant street life.

Across 7 days you will visit Grand Palace, Wat Arun and Chatuchak Weekend Market. The pace is balanced: guided highlights in the morning, free time in the afternoon.
Most days involve light walking on flat ground, so no special fitness is required. The minimum age for this departure is 0, and groups are capped at 12 travellers so the guide can give everyone attention.

Food is part of the experience: you will have the chance to try Pad Thai, Tom Yum Goong and Mango Sticky Rice. Breakfast is served at the hotel every morning, and several lunches are included while you are out with the guide.

The best months to travel are November, December and January, when the weather suits the activities in this itinerary. The price of 7000 USD per adult covers accommodation, transport on the ground, an English-speaking guide and entrance fees to the sites listed above.

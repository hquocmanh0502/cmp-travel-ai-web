TOUR: Valencia - Spain

Description:
Valencia, Spain — a 3-day journey focused on local culture, beaches, shopping and wellness.

Valencia, located on the southeastern coast of Spain, is famous for its futuristic architecture, delicious cuisine, and lively street festivals.

Across 3 days you will visit City of Arts and Sciences, Mercado Central and Bioparc Valencia. The pace is deliberately relaxed, with unhurried mornings and plenty of free time.
Most days involve light walking on flat ground, so no special fitness is required. The minimum age for this departure is 0, and groups are capped at 20 travellers so the guide can give everyone attention.

Food is part of the experience: you will have the chance to try Paella, Horchata and Buñuelos. Breakfast is served at the hotel every morning, and several lunches are included while you are out with the guide.

The best months to travel are April, May and June, when the weather suits the activities in this itinerary. The price of 4000 USD per adult covers accommodation, transport on the ground, an English-speaking guide and entrance fees to the sites listed above.

TOUR: Tokyo - Japan

Description:
Tokyo, Japan — a 5-day journey focused on history, architecture, shopping and food.

Tokyo, the capital city, is a bustling metropolis known for its modernity, ancient traditions, and vibrant pop culture.

Across 5 days you will visit Senso-ji Temple, Shibuya Crossing and Tsukiji Outer Market. The pace is balanced: guided highlights in the morning, free time in the afternoon.
Most days involve light walking on flat ground, so no special fitness is required. The minimum age for this departure is 0, and groups are capped at 21 travellers so the guide can give everyone attention.

Food is part of the experience: you will have the chance to try Sushi, Ramen and Tempura. Breakfast is served at the hotel every morning, and several lunches are included while you are out with the guide.

The best months to travel are March, April and May, when the weather suits the activities in this itinerary. The price of 5000 USD per adult covers accommodation, transport on the ground, an English-speaking guide and entrance fees to the sites listed above.

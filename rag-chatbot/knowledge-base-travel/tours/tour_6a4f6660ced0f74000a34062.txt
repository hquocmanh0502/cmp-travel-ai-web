TOUR: Langkawi - Malaysia

Description:
Langkawi, Malaysia — a 5-day journey focused on architecture, nature, beaches and food.

Langkawi is a tropical paradise with beautiful beaches, lush rainforests, and a laid-back atmosphere, perfect for relaxation and adventure.

Across 5 days you will visit Langkawi Cable Car, Eagle Square and Tanah Rata Waterfall. The pace is deliberately relaxed, with unhurried mornings and plenty of free time.
Expect a few hours of walking each day, including some uneven ground and stairs. The minimum age for this departure is 6, and groups are capped at 20 travellers so the guide can give everyone attention.

Food is part of the experience: you will have the chance to try Nasi Dagang, Laksa and Satay. Breakfast is served at the hotel every morning, and several lunches are included while you are out with the guide.

The best months to travel are November, December and January, when the weather suits the activities in this itinerary. The price of 5000 USD per adult covers accommodation, transport on the ground, an English-speaking guide and entrance fees to the sites listed above.

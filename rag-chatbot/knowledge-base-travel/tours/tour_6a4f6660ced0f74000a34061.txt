TOUR: Malacca - Malaysia

Description:
Malacca, Malaysia — a 3-day journey focused on history, local culture, mountain scenery and shopping.

Malacca (Melaka) is a historical city with a rich colonial past, featuring well-preserved heritage buildings and a unique blend of cultures.

Across 3 days you will visit St. Paul's Hill, Jonker Street and A Famosa. The pace is full, designed for travellers who want to see as much as possible.
Some days involve long walks at altitude or on steep trails, so good fitness matters. The minimum age for this departure is 12, and groups are capped at 10 travellers so the guide can give everyone attention.

Food is part of the experience: you will have the chance to try Chicken Rice Ball, Nyonya Laksa and Cendol. Breakfast is served at the hotel every morning, and several lunches are included while you are out with the guide.

The best months to travel are November, December and January, when the weather suits the activities in this itinerary. The price of 3200 USD per adult covers accommodation, transport on the ground, an English-speaking guide and entrance fees to the sites listed above.

TOUR: Marseille - France

Description:
Marseille, France — a 5-day journey focused on nature, beaches, food and great value.

Marseille, located on the Mediterranean coast, is the second-largest city in France and is known for its rich history, vibrant culture, and beautiful waterfront.

Across 5 days you will visit Old Port, Notre-Dame de la Garde and Calanques National Park. The pace is deliberately relaxed, with unhurried mornings and plenty of free time.
Expect a few hours of walking each day, including some uneven ground and stairs. The minimum age for this departure is 6, and groups are capped at 23 travellers so the guide can give everyone attention.

Food is part of the experience: you will have the chance to try Bouillabaisse, Navettes and Pastis. Breakfast is served at the hotel every morning, and several lunches are included while you are out with the guide.

The best months to travel are April, May and June, when the weather suits the activities in this itinerary. The price of 5000 USD per adult covers accommodation, transport on the ground, an English-speaking guide and entrance fees to the sites listed above.

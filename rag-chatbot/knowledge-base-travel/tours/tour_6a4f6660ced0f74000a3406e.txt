TOUR: Berlin - Germany

Description:
Berlin, Germany — a 7-day journey focused on museums, island life, food and premium comfort.

Berlin, the capital city of Germany, is known for its rich history, vibrant cultural scene, and iconic landmarks.

Across 7 days you will visit Brandenburg Gate, Berlin Wall and Museum Island. The pace is balanced: guided highlights in the morning, free time in the afternoon.
Most days involve light walking on flat ground, so no special fitness is required. The minimum age for this departure is 0, and groups are capped at 12 travellers so the guide can give everyone attention.

Food is part of the experience: you will have the chance to try Currywurst, Schnitzel and Berliner Pfannkuchen. Breakfast is served at the hotel every morning, and several lunches are included while you are out with the guide.

The best months to travel are April, May and June, when the weather suits the activities in this itinerary. The price of 6000 USD per adult covers accommodation, transport on the ground, an English-speaking guide and entrance fees to the sites listed above.

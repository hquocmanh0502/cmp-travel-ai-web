TOUR: Maldives - Maldives

Description:
Maldives, Maldives — a 5-day journey focused on beaches, island life, food and great value.

The Maldives is a tropical paradise renowned for its luxurious resorts, and vibrant marine life, making it a top destination for beach lovers and divers.

Across 5 days you will visit Male, Ari Atoll   and Bioluminescent Beaches. The pace is deliberately relaxed, with unhurried mornings and plenty of free time.
Most days involve light walking on flat ground, so no special fitness is required. The minimum age for this departure is 0, and groups are capped at 20 travellers so the guide can give everyone attention.

Food is part of the experience: you will have the chance to try Mas Huni, Garudhiya and Bis Keemiya. Breakfast is served at the hotel every morning, and several lunches are included while you are out with the guide.

The best months to travel are November, December and January, when the weather suits the activities in this itinerary. The price of 5000 USD per adult covers accommodation, transport on the ground, an English-speaking guide and entrance fees to the sites listed above.

TOUR: Toronto - Canada

Description:
Toronto, Canada — a 5-day journey focused on museums, history, architecture and local culture.

Toronto is Canada's largest city, a vibrant metropolis with diverse neighborhoods, iconic landmarks like the CN Tower, and a thriving arts and culture scene.

Across 5 days you will visit CN Tower, Royal Ontario Museum and Distillery District. The pace is balanced: guided highlights in the morning, free time in the afternoon.
Most days involve light walking on flat ground, so no special fitness is required. The minimum age for this departure is 0, and groups are capped at 17 travellers so the guide can give everyone attention.

Food is part of the experience: you will have the chance to try Poutine, Peameal Bacon Sandwich and Butter Tarts. Breakfast is served at the hotel every morning, and several lunches are included while you are out with the guide.

The best months to travel are June, July and August, when the weather suits the activities in this itinerary. The price of 4500 USD per adult covers accommodation, transport on the ground, an English-speaking guide and entrance fees to the sites listed above.

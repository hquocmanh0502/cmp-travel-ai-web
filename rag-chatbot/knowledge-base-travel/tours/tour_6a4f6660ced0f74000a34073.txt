TOUR: Milan - Italy

Description:
Milan, Italy — a 5-day journey focused on food and great value.

Milan, the fashion capital of Italy, is known for its stylish boutiques, stunning architecture, and vibrant arts scene. It's a bustling metropolis with a cosmopolitan vibe.

Across 5 days you will visit Duomo, Galleria Vittorio Emanuele II and The Last Supper. The pace is balanced: guided highlights in the morning, free time in the afternoon.
Most days involve light walking on flat ground, so no special fitness is required. The minimum age for this departure is 0, and groups are capped at 21 travellers so the guide can give everyone attention.

Food is part of the experience: you will have the chance to try Risotto alla Milanese, Cotoletta alla Milanese and Panettone. Breakfast is served at the hotel every morning, and several lunches are included while you are out with the guide.

The best months to travel are April, May and June, when the weather suits the activities in this itinerary. The price of 4500 USD per adult covers accommodation, transport on the ground, an English-speaking guide and entrance fees to the sites listed above.

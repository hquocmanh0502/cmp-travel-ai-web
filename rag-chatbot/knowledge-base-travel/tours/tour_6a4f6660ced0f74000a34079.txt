TOUR: Barcelona - Spain

Description:
Barcelona, Spain — a 7-day journey focused on architecture, local culture, nature and beaches.

Barcelona, located on the northeastern coast of Spain, is known for its unique architecture, vibrant street life, and beautiful beaches.

Across 7 days you will visit Sagrada Familia, Park Güell and Gothic Quarter. The pace is deliberately relaxed, with unhurried mornings and plenty of free time.
Expect a few hours of walking each day, including some uneven ground and stairs. The minimum age for this departure is 6, and groups are capped at 12 travellers so the guide can give everyone attention.

Food is part of the experience: you will have the chance to try Tapas, Paella and Churros. Breakfast is served at the hotel every morning, and several lunches are included while you are out with the guide.

The best months to travel are April, May and June, when the weather suits the activities in this itinerary. The price of 6500 USD per adult covers accommodation, transport on the ground, an English-speaking guide and entrance fees to the sites listed above.

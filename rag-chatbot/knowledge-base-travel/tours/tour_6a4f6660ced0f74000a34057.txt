TOUR: Lauterbrunnen - Switzerland

Description:
Lauterbrunnen, Switzerland — a 5-day journey focused on history, architecture, nature and mountain scenery.

Lauterbrunnen, in the Swiss Alps, is known for its stunning waterfalls and scenic beauty. It's a serene valley with lush meadows and towering mountains, perfect for nature lovers.

Across 5 days you will visit Staubbach Falls, Lauterbrunnen Valley and Trümmelbach Falls. The pace is balanced: guided highlights in the morning, free time in the afternoon.
Some days involve long walks at altitude or on steep trails, so good fitness matters. The minimum age for this departure is 12, and groups are capped at 12 travellers so the guide can give everyone attention.

Food is part of the experience: you will have the chance to try Swiss Fondue, Raclette and Hot Chocolate. Breakfast is served at the hotel every morning, and several lunches are included while you are out with the guide.

The best months to travel are June, July and August, when the weather suits the activities in this itinerary. The price of 5500 USD per adult covers accommodation, transport on the ground, an English-speaking guide and entrance fees to the sites listed above.

TOUR: Santorini - Greece

Description:
Santorini, Greece — a 5-day journey focused on local culture, beaches, island life and food.

Santorini is a stunning volcanic island known for its white-washed buildings, dramatic sunsets, and picturesque cliffside villages.

Across 5 days you will visit Oia, Fira and Red Beach. The pace is deliberately relaxed, with unhurried mornings and plenty of free time.
Most days involve light walking on flat ground, so no special fitness is required. The minimum age for this departure is 0, and groups are capped at 12 travellers so the guide can give everyone attention.

Food is part of the experience: you will have the chance to try Fava, Santorini Tomatokeftedes and Loukoumades. Breakfast is served at the hotel every morning, and several lunches are included while you are out with the guide.

The best months to travel are April, May and June, when the weather suits the activities in this itinerary. The price of 5500 USD per adult covers accommodation, transport on the ground, an English-speaking guide and entrance fees to the sites listed above.

TOUR: Manchester - United Kingdom

Description:
Manchester, United Kingdom — a 5-day journey focused on architecture, local culture, nightlife and food.

Manchester, a vibrant city in England, is renowned for its rich industrial heritage, world-class music scene, and famous football clubs.

Across 5 days you will visit Old Trafford Stadium, Manchester Cathedral and The Lowry. The pace is balanced: guided highlights in the morning, free time in the afternoon.
Most days involve light walking on flat ground, so no special fitness is required. The minimum age for this departure is 0, and groups are capped at 16 travellers so the guide can give everyone attention.

Food is part of the experience: you will have the chance to try Lancashire Hotpot, Manchester Tart and Fisherman's Breakfast. Breakfast is served at the hotel every morning, and several lunches are included while you are out with the guide.

The best months to travel are April, May and June, when the weather suits the activities in this itinerary. The price of 5000 USD per adult covers accommodation, transport on the ground, an English-speaking guide and entrance fees to the sites listed above.

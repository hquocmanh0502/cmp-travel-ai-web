TOUR: Lisbon - Portugal

Description:
Lisbon, Portugal — a 3-day journey focused on history, architecture, local culture and shopping.

Lisbon, the capital city of Portugal, is a charming destination with narrow cobblestone streets, colorful buildings, and stunning viewpoints over the Tagus River.

Across 3 days you will visit Belém Tower, Jerónimos Monastery and Castelo de São Jorge. The pace is full, designed for travellers who want to see as much as possible.
Most days involve light walking on flat ground, so no special fitness is required. The minimum age for this departure is 0, and groups are capped at 24 travellers so the guide can give everyone attention.

Food is part of the experience: you will have the chance to try Pastéis de Nata, Bacalhau and Sardines. Breakfast is served at the hotel every morning, and several lunches are included while you are out with the guide.

The best months to travel are April, May and June, when the weather suits the activities in this itinerary. The price of 4000 USD per adult covers accommodation, transport on the ground, an English-speaking guide and entrance fees to the sites listed above.

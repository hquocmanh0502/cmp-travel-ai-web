TOUR: Vancouver - Canada

Description:
Vancouver, Canada — a 5-day journey focused on architecture, nature, mountain scenery and beaches.

Vancouver is a coastal city known for its stunning natural beauty, surrounded by mountains and ocean, offering a mix of outdoor activities and urban experiences.

Across 5 days you will visit Stanley Park, Granville Island and Capilano Suspension Bridge. The pace is deliberately relaxed, with unhurried mornings and plenty of free time.
Some days involve long walks at altitude or on steep trails, so good fitness matters. The minimum age for this departure is 12, and groups are capped at 10 travellers so the guide can give everyone attention.

Food is part of the experience: you will have the chance to try Salmon, Japadog and Nanaimo Bar. Breakfast is served at the hotel every morning, and several lunches are included while you are out with the guide.

The best months to travel are June, July and August, when the weather suits the activities in this itinerary. The price of 5000 USD per adult covers accommodation, transport on the ground, an English-speaking guide and entrance fees to the sites listed above.

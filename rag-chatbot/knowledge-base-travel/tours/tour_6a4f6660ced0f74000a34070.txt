TOUR: Rome - Italy

Description:
Rome, Italy — a 7-day journey focused on history, architecture, shopping and food.

Rome, the capital city of Italy, is a treasure trove of ancient ruins, majestic churches, and exquisite art. It's known for its rich history and vibrant street life.

Across 7 days you will visit Colosseum, Vatican City and Trevi Fountain. The pace is balanced: guided highlights in the morning, free time in the afternoon.
Most days involve light walking on flat ground, so no special fitness is required. The minimum age for this departure is 0, and groups are capped at 12 travellers so the guide can give everyone attention.

Food is part of the experience: you will have the chance to try Pizza, Pasta Carbonara and Gelato. Breakfast is served at the hotel every morning, and several lunches are included while you are out with the guide.

The best months to travel are April, May and June, when the weather suits the activities in this itinerary. The price of 6000 USD per adult covers accommodation, transport on the ground, an English-speaking guide and entrance fees to the sites listed above.

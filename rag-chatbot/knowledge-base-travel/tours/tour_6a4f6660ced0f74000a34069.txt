TOUR: Delhi - India

Description:
Delhi, India — a 5-day journey focused on history, shopping, food and great value.

Delhi, the capital city of India, is a historical and cultural hub known for its magnificent architecture, bustling markets, and diverse cuisine.

Across 5 days you will visit Red Fort, Qutub Minar and India Gate. The pace is balanced: guided highlights in the morning, free time in the afternoon.
Most days involve light walking on flat ground, so no special fitness is required. The minimum age for this departure is 0, and groups are capped at 21 travellers so the guide can give everyone attention.

Food is part of the experience: you will have the chance to try Paratha, Chole Bhature and Butter Chicken. Breakfast is served at the hotel every morning, and several lunches are included while you are out with the guide.

The best months to travel are November, December and January, when the weather suits the activities in this itinerary. The price of 5000 USD per adult covers accommodation, transport on the ground, an English-speaking guide and entrance fees to the sites listed above.

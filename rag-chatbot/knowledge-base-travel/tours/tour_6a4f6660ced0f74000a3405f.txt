TOUR: Kazan - Russia

Description:
Kazan, Russia — a 3-day journey focused on history, architecture, shopping and food.

Kazan is a city where East meets West, showcasing its unique blend of Islamic and Russian cultures, with a stunning Kremlin and a vibrant city center.

Across 3 days you will visit Kazan Kremlin, Kul Sharif Mosque and Bauman Street. The pace is full, designed for travellers who want to see as much as possible.
Most days involve light walking on flat ground, so no special fitness is required. The minimum age for this departure is 0, and groups are capped at 21 travellers so the guide can give everyone attention.

Food is part of the experience: you will have the chance to try Chak-Chak, Tatar Plow and Ikra. Breakfast is served at the hotel every morning, and several lunches are included while you are out with the guide.

The best months to travel are May, June and July, when the weather suits the activities in this itinerary. The price of 3800 USD per adult covers accommodation, transport on the ground, an English-speaking guide and entrance fees to the sites listed above.

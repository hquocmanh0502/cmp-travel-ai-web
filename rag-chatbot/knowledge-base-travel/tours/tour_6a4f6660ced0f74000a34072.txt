TOUR: Venice - Italy

Description:
Venice, Italy — a 5-day journey focused on history, architecture, food and great value.

Venice, a city built on water, is famous for its canals, romantic gondolas, and stunning architecture. It's a unique and enchanting destination in Italy.

Across 5 days you will visit St. Mark's Square, Rialto Bridge and Doge's Palace. The pace is balanced: guided highlights in the morning, free time in the afternoon.
Most days involve light walking on flat ground, so no special fitness is required. The minimum age for this departure is 0, and groups are capped at 24 travellers so the guide can give everyone attention.

Food is part of the experience: you will have the chance to try Seafood Risotto, Cicchetti and Tiramisu. Breakfast is served at the hotel every morning, and several lunches are included while you are out with the guide.

The best months to travel are April, May and June, when the weather suits the activities in this itinerary. The price of 5000 USD per adult covers accommodation, transport on the ground, an English-speaking guide and entrance fees to the sites listed above.

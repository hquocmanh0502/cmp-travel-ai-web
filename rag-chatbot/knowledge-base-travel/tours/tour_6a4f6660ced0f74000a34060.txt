TOUR: Kuala Lumpur - Malaysia

Description:
Kuala Lumpur, Malaysia — a 5-day journey focused on history, architecture, local culture and photography.

Kuala Lumpur is the vibrant capital city of Malaysia, famous for its modern skyline, iconic Petronas Twin Towers, and diverse cultural heritage.

Across 5 days you will visit Petronas Twin Towers, Batu Caves and Merdeka Square. The pace is balanced: guided highlights in the morning, free time in the afternoon.
Most days involve light walking on flat ground, so no special fitness is required. The minimum age for this departure is 0, and groups are capped at 22 travellers so the guide can give everyone attention.

Food is part of the experience: you will have the chance to try Nasi Lemak, Char Kway Teow and Roti Canai. Breakfast is served at the hotel every morning, and several lunches are included while you are out with the guide.

The best months to travel are November, December and January, when the weather suits the activities in this itinerary. The price of 4500 USD per adult covers accommodation, transport on the ground, an English-speaking guide and entrance fees to the sites listed above.

TOUR: Kyoto - Japan

Description:
Kyoto, Japan — a 5-day journey focused on architecture, local culture, food and great value.

Kyoto is a city with a rich history, showcasing stunning temples, traditional tea houses, and beautiful cherry blossoms in spring.

Across 5 days you will visit Kinkaku-ji (Golden Pavilion), Fushimi Inari Taisha and Arashiyama Bamboo Grove. The pace is balanced: guided highlights in the morning, free time in the afternoon.
Most days involve light walking on flat ground, so no special fitness is required. The minimum age for this departure is 0, and groups are capped at 21 travellers so the guide can give everyone attention.

Food is part of the experience: you will have the chance to try Kaiseki Ryori, Matcha Green Tea and Yudofu (Tofu Hot Pot). Breakfast is served at the hotel every morning, and several lunches are included while you are out with the guide.

The best months to travel are March, April and May, when the weather suits the activities in this itinerary. The price of 4800 USD per adult covers accommodation, transport on the ground, an English-speaking guide and entrance fees to the sites listed above.

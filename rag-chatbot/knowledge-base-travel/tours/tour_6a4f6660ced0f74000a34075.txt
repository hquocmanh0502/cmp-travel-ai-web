TOUR: Paris - France

Description:
Paris, France — a 7-day journey focused on museums, history, architecture and food.

Paris, the capital city of France, is known as the 'City of Love' and is famous for its iconic landmarks, rich history, and exquisite cuisine.

Across 7 days you will visit Eiffel Tower, Louvre Museum and Notre-Dame Cathedral. The pace is balanced: guided highlights in the morning, free time in the afternoon.
Most days involve light walking on flat ground, so no special fitness is required. The minimum age for this departure is 0, and groups are capped at 12 travellers so the guide can give everyone attention.

Food is part of the experience: you will have the chance to try Croissant, Escargot and Macarons. Breakfast is served at the hotel every morning, and several lunches are included while you are out with the guide.

The best months to travel are April, May and June, when the weather suits the activities in this itinerary. The price of 7000 USD per adult covers accommodation, transport on the ground, an English-speaking guide and entrance fees to the sites listed above.
